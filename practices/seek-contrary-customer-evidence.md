# Seek contrary evidence in customer material

## Use When

You have a favored strategy and actual interviews or feedback you can use.

## Try It

1. State your strategy and identify the customer records being compared.
2. Ask AI where the records conflict with the strategy, not just where they agree.
3. Read the original passages and, where needed, follow up with customers.
4. Decide whether to revise the strategy or investigate further.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Require locators for every claimed contradiction and retain uncertainty; compare with an alternative only when useful.
