# Rehearse a talk by compressing and expanding it

## Use When

You know the topic but the talk is cluttered or hard to deliver without a script.

## Try It

1. Speak a rough three-minute version.
2. Reflect on what mattered, then repeat in two minutes, one minute and thirty seconds.
3. Repeat the short version, then expand back through one, two and three minutes.
4. Add back only supporting material you choose; keep a few main-point cues and rehearse with a person.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** The times are the author’s examples, not a validated dose. Preserve any needed accessibility support or mandatory wording.
