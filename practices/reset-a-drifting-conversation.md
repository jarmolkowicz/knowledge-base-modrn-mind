# Reset a drifting conversation

## Use When

Repeated corrections have obscured the intended task.

## Try It

1. Write your own brief of the result, missing constraints and observed failure. Treat AI failure explanations as suggestions.
2. Start a fresh conversation with the corrected brief and necessary context; ask about unresolved gaps first.
3. Answer and assess against the brief. Stop if evidence or expertise is still missing.
