# Record a lived moment each day

## Use When

You want material drawn from your experience rather than generated personal stories.

## Try It

1. Briefly note a moment that caught your attention, with its date.
2. Record during the day or reflect at its end; a few words are enough.
3. Mark older memories as memories so they do not appear to have happened that day.
4. Return to the notes and choose which, if any, are worth developing into stories.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Keep observations separate from interpretations of other people. AI may help search the archive later, but must not invent your experiences.
