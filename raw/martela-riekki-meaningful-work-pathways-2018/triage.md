## Summary

The authors test four proposed pathways to meaningful work: autonomy, competence, relatedness and beneficence, or perceived positive contribution. The four were independently associated with meaning in Finland and India; competence was not independently significant in the US model controlling for the other three.

These findings provide a cross-country foundation for discussing meaning beyond efficiency. The word 'pathway' is theoretical: cross-sectional regression and mediation do not establish causal routes. Competence here is felt efficacy, not independently assessed capability.

- Limits: Self-reports, different meaningful-work scales across studies, limited country coverage and no causal identification. Retain the US competence exception.
