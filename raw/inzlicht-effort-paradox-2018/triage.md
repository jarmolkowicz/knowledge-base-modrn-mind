## Summary

The review argues that effort is both costly and sometimes valued. People may value outcomes more after investing effort and may sometimes seek effort itself. This qualifies a simple account in which less effort is always preferred.

The authors distinguish effort-related valuation from mere willingness to work for a larger reward. Their discussion of training willingness to exert effort is a research direction, not a demonstrated general solution for lasting learning or AI dependency.
