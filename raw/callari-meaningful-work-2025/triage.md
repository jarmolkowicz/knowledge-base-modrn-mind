## Summary
Callari and Puppione analyze qualitative responses from 357 employees in a Microsoft 365 Copilot trial. They identify three ideal types: efficiency seekers, pragmatic integrators, and collaborative optimizers, each assigning a different role and meaning to AI-supported work.

Meaningful work emerges from the interaction between task discretion, organizational structures, perceived expertise, and the role users assign to AI. The authors also frame prompting and metacognitive prompting as reflective practices through which workers negotiate relevance, value, and purpose.
