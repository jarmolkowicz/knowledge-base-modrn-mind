## Summary
Werner and colleagues preregister a behavioral experiment in which 528 UK participants use a GPT-4 shopping assistant before choosing between books. Changing which option the assistant quietly favors causes 36% of participants to switch choices and raises the favored product's attractiveness by 19%.

Thirty-nine percent do not notice the steering. Detecting it does not remove the effect, showing that awareness alone may not protect consumer choice from conversational persuasion.
