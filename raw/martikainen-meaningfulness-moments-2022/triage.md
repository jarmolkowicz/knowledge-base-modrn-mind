## Summary

The authors distinguish affective experiences of meaning from job attributes and broad evaluations. They analyze narrative tone, themes and structure, describing connection, contribution and growth alongside confinement and static accounts of meaninglessness.

This supports a narrower claim about how participants described experience. Retrospective narratives are not direct real-time measures of feelings, and the practical suggestions are not evaluated interventions.
