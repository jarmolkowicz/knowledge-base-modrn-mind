# Ask what an update would change

## Use When

You are drawn into comparing models, frameworks or AI news while trying to finish a real piece of work.

## Try It

1. Name the decision the new information could change.
2. Estimate how much difference the best technical choice would make compared with a good-enough existing choice. Mark what you do not know.
3. Ask how difficult switching away from the new choice would be.
4. Investigate further when the likely gain or required obligation warrants it; otherwise defer. [Inference] Record what failure, change in need or maintenance deadline would make you revisit.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis / observed or reported:** Practitioner advice drawn from discussions about AI product building; no measured reduction in distraction or improvement in technical choices.

**Related research:** Related to [[effort-investment-review]]: effort allocated to tools is part of work, not free overhead. No validation of this heuristic reviewed.

**Untested:** Accuracy of estimated improvement and whether deferring investigation improves the outcome.

## Limits

A small immediate gain can conceal a longer-term compatibility, security or accessibility need. This is a decision filter, not a rule to ignore updates. Avoid pretending an uncertain estimate is a precise calculation.
