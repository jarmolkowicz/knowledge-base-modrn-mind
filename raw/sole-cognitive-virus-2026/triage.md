## Summary

The paper models how social transmission and reinforcement could produce nonlinear transitions toward persistent LLM dependence. The viral analogy describes a proposed population process; it is not a medical diagnosis or proof that ordinary AI use is pathological.

The discussion distinguishes AI that extends competence from AI that substitutes for the activity maintaining it. Its tipping points and recovery conditions follow from a simplified model. They do not establish observed population thresholds, real-world prevalence, irreversible decline or a measured magnitude of skill loss.
