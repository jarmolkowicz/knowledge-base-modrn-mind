## Summary

Chen and colleagues distinguish satisfaction of autonomy, competence and relatedness from active frustration of those needs. They examine associations with wellbeing and ill-being and whether these vary by country or reported need strength.

The findings support the framework in the sampled settings, but do not establish universality for every culture or person. The authors acknowledge student convenience samples and cross-sectional data. Felt competence is not a test of unaided ability.
