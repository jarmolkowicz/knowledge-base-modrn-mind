# Speak your thoughts before summarizing them with AI

## Use When

Messages and tasks crowd your attention and you need to hear your own priorities.

Author’s intended benefit: Articulate concerns before organizing them with AI.

## Try It

1. Record or dictate what is on your mind; Perkins does this during a walk.
2. Ask AI to summarize the resulting notes.
3. Use the summary to identify possible actions and a broader view.
4. [Inference] Compare against your original notes and choose the actions yourself; correct omitted concerns or invented commitments. A seated voice note or written note is an alternative to walking.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** First-person routine.

**Observed or reported:** Reports dictating her thoughts during walks, then summarizing them to identify actions and gain perspective.

**Related research:** No directly relevant evaluation of this routine was identified in this review. The practitioner account above supplies its rationale; the AI adaptation remains untested.

**Untested:** Whether this specific procedure improves judgment, independent capability, creative quality or calibration beyond the reported experience. A completed artifact, more options and felt clarity are different outcomes.

## Limits

- The adjacent AI-first ideation habit is a different sequence and not evidence for thinking first.
- A summary can omit a concern or turn speculation into a commitment.
- Walking is optional; access and privacy conditions vary.

## What to Notice

[Inference] Notice whether the action list reflects your priorities rather than merely the easiest items to summarize.
