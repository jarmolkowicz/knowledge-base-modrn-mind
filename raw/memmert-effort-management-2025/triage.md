## Summary
Memmert, Soroko, and Bittner interview 21 knowledge workers who frequently use GenAI. Rather than simply reducing effort, most participants strategically move effort between drafting, prompting, checking, learning, and integrating outputs.

Workers often see themselves as process administrators. The study argues that social-loafing models transfer poorly to human-AI dyads because AI can reduce effort in one subtask while increasing verification and coordination work elsewhere.
