## Summary

Martela and Steger distinguish coherence (life making sense), purpose (goals and direction) and significance (life being worthwhile). Their contribution is conceptual precision, especially against treating all uses of 'meaning' as interchangeable.

They propose relationships and a research agenda for measuring the facets separately. This paper does not itself validate a three-part diagnostic tool, show effects of AI on meaning, or establish that life-level and work-level meaning are the same.
