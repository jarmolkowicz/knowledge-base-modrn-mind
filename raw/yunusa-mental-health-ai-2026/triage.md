Yunusa and colleagues present a broad review of AI, mental health, work, and social relations, framed through socio-technical systems theory. The paper states that it follows PRISMA and draws from several academic databases, but its conclusions remain broad and largely repeat established claims. It offers general human-centered policy recommendations rather than a distinct mechanism or strong new finding.
