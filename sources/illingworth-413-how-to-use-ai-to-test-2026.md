# How to Use AI to Test Whether a Career Decision Is Reversible

## Citation

Ting Wang (Tzu-Ting), with Sam Illingworth. (2026-02-17). How to Use AI to Test Whether a Career Decision Is Reversible. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/ai-career-decision-reversible-return-path

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to make practical reversibility and constraints explicit.

## Key Findings

Wang reports that naming the cost of returning reduced felt pressure during a career choice; the account does not isolate AI’s contribution.

**Locators:** L24, attribution; L30–40, return-path prompt; L44–58, account and reflection.

## Evidence

**Basis:** First-person career account plus proposed AI inquiry.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 98 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- Visa, tax, pension and employment facts need authoritative checking; none is established by the prompt.
- AI must not reclassify a structural constraint as merely emotional.
- A return route can change after the decision.
