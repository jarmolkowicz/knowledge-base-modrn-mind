## Summary
Buijsman, Carter, and Bermudez analyze how AI decision support affects domain-specific autonomy. They separate skilled competence from authentic value formation and argue that AI can undermine either immediately or gradually, including through unreliable failure signals and unnoticed shifts in user preferences.

The paper proposes autonomy-preserving design patterns: specify the human and AI roles, expose defeaters and uncertainty, preserve meaningful choice, support learning, and monitor longer-term changes in competence and values.
