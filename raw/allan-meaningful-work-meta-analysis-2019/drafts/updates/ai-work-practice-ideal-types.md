## Proposed Additions

### To "Strengths / Limitations"

[[meaningful-work|Meaningful work]] should be assessed separately from engagement, satisfaction and performance. Allan et al. (2019) found correlations among these outcomes, not interchangeability or evidence that a particular AI adoption orientation causes them. Do not use increased adoption as a proxy for increased meaning.

### To "Related"

- [[meaningful-work]] — separate outcome, not a maturity score.
