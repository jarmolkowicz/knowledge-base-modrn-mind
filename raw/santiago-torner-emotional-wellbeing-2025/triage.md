## Summary
Santiago-Torner reviews 40 articles from 2020-2025 on AI, emotional support, mental health, trust, authenticity, and autonomy. The paper presents AI as an ambivalent relational influence: it can expand access and support self-regulation while also creating dependency, depersonalization, and weaker human bonds.

Its proposed "digital affective paradox" says increased digital accompaniment need not create genuine connection. The synthesis is broad and largely repeats claims covered by stronger direct studies.
