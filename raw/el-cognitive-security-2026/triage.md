## Summary
El and colleagues define cognitive security as protecting human cognitive processes from hazardous influence by AI. They organize emerging influence capabilities and argue that conventional AI safety work insufficiently covers effects on beliefs, preferences, decisions, and behavior.

The paper proposes a research roadmap built around threat models, attack and defense mechanisms, and measures of cognitive impact. It is a position paper and framework, not direct evidence that the proposed risks occur at a given scale.
