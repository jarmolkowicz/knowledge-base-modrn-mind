## Summary
Schmutz and colleagues review human-AI team constellations, trust, and shared cognition. Adding AI often reduces coordination and communication, while trust declines after users begin with inflated capability expectations.

Human-AI teams can therefore underperform despite strong component capabilities. Mutual understanding, shared mental models, and calibrated trust are presented as key unresolved conditions for useful teaming.
