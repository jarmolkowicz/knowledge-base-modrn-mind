# How to Hold on to What Should Stay Human

## Citation

Timo Mason and Sam Illingworth. How to Hold on to What Should Stay Human. 2025-11-13. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/when-automation-tempts-you

## Type and account basis

Timo Mason’s first-person prompt experiment plus a one-week practice proposed with Sam Illingworth.

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to make a personal non-delegation choice visible and enact it.

## Key Findings

Different stated business purposes elicited different suggested protected tasks; connection with people recurred.
