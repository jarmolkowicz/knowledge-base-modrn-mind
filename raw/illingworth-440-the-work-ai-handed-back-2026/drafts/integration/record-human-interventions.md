# Record the human work needed around AI

## Use When

Important corrections or contextual decisions disappear from the visible output.

## Try It

1. Keep a private dated note when you catch an error, intervene or give contextual advice.
2. Record the action and what happened next.
3. Use the record when discussing the work required, with relevant people.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Separate observed result from estimated damage avoided or costs saved. Do not put confidential client details in an unauthorized log.
