# Develop parallel creative routes before combining them

## Use When

A team wants to compare what human-led and AI-supported workflows contribute to the same challenge.

Author’s intended benefit: Preserve alternative framings and make human/AI contributions visible.

## Try It

1. Give two groups the same brief and relevant context.
2. Let one develop a human-led route and the other a heavily AI-supported route separately, following the reported consultancy example.
3. Bring the resulting routes together and compare their coherence, narrative and contextual fit, as well as the breadth of generated possibilities.
4. Choose or combine elements using the team’s judgment. [Inference] Preserve the initial routes and reasons for selection so differences remain inspectable.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** Management synthesis drawing on studies, interviews and reported organizational experiments.

**Observed or reported:** Reports a With Company branding comparison: human-led and heavily AI-supported teams worked in parallel, with the human-led proposal selected. No randomized comparison or retained-skill test.

**Related research:** Anderson and colleagues found group-level similarity in a small creative-ideation comparison. It does not test this team routine or establish retained expertise. See [[anderson-homogenization-2024]]. This is context, not direct validation.

**Untested:** Whether this specific procedure improves judgment, independent capability, creative quality or calibration beyond the reported experience. A completed artifact, more options and felt clarity are different outcomes.

**Related research, not validation:** Doshi and Hauser found that access to AI story ideas improved average evaluations of individual short stories while stories within the AI-assisted conditions became more similar to one another (PDF pp.4–5). This was an individual writing experiment, not a test of parallel teams, retained creative ability or downstream implementation.

### Additional research context

[Inference] In a parallel-workflow comparison, inspect the range of resulting ideas separately from the quality of the chosen artifact. Preserve reasons for selection. Neither more options nor a better final artifact alone demonstrates that people became more creative. The Mascareño stage-comparison lead is not used here: its stronger canonical interpretation still needs a separate original-methods review.

## Limits

- The experiment compared two team workflows, not a pure AI system against humans.
- Reported counts of images and programs are not evidence of lasting reasoning gains.
- Underlying studies and programs need their own review before using their claims as primary evidence.

## What to Notice

[Inference] Notice which workflow contributed a useful framing and who can explain it. More images is different from a better proposal or preserved capability.
