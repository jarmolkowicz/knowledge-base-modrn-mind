## Summary
Topaz and colleagues audit 2.47 million open-access biomedical papers and 97.1 million PubMed-identified references published from 2023 to early 2026. They identify 4,046 fabricated references across 2,810 papers.

The affected-paper rate rises from about one in 2,828 in 2023 to one in 277 in early 2026. The timing is consistent with LLM-assisted writing, but the study cannot establish AI as the cause; paper mills and indexing changes may also contribute.
