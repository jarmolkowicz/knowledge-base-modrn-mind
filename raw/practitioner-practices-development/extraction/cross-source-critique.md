# Cross-source critique and consolidation

2026-09-26. Applies to the completed selected-source review; draft-only. Source IDs refer to [the review register](source-review-register.md).

## Trust belongs to a claim and a context

Publication reputation and professional experience are reasons to inspect a source, not reasons to accept all its claims. This pool includes named guest contributors, company leaders reporting their own practices, course sellers, newsletter demonstrations and interview anecdotes. Each supplies a different basis.

Keep these distinctions visible:

- Hogue, Carver, Khalaf and other guest contributors get credit separately from the newsletter host.
- Multiple posts by one author are variants, not independent replications. Repeated ideas in one podcast network do not establish coverage across professions.
- A detailed demonstration establishes that the procedure can be described or attempted. It does not establish reliable effects.
- A practitioner can report useful experience while making unsupported causal, clinical, environmental or numerical claims. Retain the bounded contribution and state what was excluded.
- Newsletter sponsorship, course promotion and founder interests do not automatically disqualify a source; they make independent outcome evidence particularly useful.

No systematic external credential verification was performed in this phase. The retained record supports attribution and the reported experience, with explicit exceptions. Do not publish a global “trusted experts” endorsement from these reviews.

These are source-specific decisions. They do not establish that every use of the same prompt is harmful or that a whole author should be rejected.

## Neighboring cards worth reviewing together

Keep cards separate where they require a different action, setting or test. Do not merge merely because both mention reflection or agency.

| Neighboring contributions | Consolidation judgment |
|---|---|
| Save your starting point; build the opposing case yourself; explain back | Shared human-first element, different work and feedback. Keep distinct; cross-reference later. |
| Criticism without rewriting; creative disagreement checklist; argument outline | One retains wording, one builds criteria, one orders a message. Keep distinct. |
| AI-generated questions; decisive-question exercise; customer contradictions | Generating questions, selecting consequential questions and testing against actual evidence are different. Do not collapse into “ask AI to challenge me.” |
| Ada Chen Rekhi advisers; Duke independent opinions; Yandell opposing case | Contextual advice, independent elicitation and live disagreement address different social problems. Retain distinct procedures. |
| PR/FAQ; explanatory model; Weinstein’s newcomer account | Keep prose proposal and quantitative/conceptual model separate; use Weinstein as supporting writing-to-learn account. |
| Winters rehearsal; Abrahams ADD; de Montebello Accordion | Audience anticipation, answer structure and compress/expand rehearsal are distinct. McAllister’s debrief and Fried’s low-stakes advice remain support. |
| Duarte listening; Crowley outline; Dill quality review | Input from people, artifact construction and evaluation of actual use happen at different stages. Keep separate. |
| Writing provenance; intervention log; decision/outcome log | Records answer different questions: how created, what human correction required, whether a choice worked. Avoid a single vague “journal” card. |
| Parallel work; multiple model reviewers; human independent attempts | Independence is not guaranteed by different windows or agents; diversity and evidential independence must be assessed. |

## Before an integrated practice is claimed to strengthen thinking

The card must name the person’s action, the assistant’s bounded contribution, a real-world check where needed, and the choice the person retains. The source must support the procedure as attributed; additions remain labeled.

An origin account can justify “worth trying with these limits.” Claims of improved unaided ability, better calibration or durable judgment need evidence beyond an attractive workflow. A future evaluation should keep assisted output, unaided performance and felt confidence separate.
