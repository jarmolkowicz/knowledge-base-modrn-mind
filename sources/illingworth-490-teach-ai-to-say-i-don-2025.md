# Teach AI to Say I Don't Know

## Citation

Sam Illingworth. (2025-08-26). Teach AI to Say I Don't Know. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/slow-ai-9-teach-it-to-say-i-dont

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to stay with an unresolved question and turn uncertainty into creative material.

## Key Findings

The author reports finding the forced non-answer more generous; no epistemic calibration measure.

**Locators:** L26–42, uncertainty instructions; L50–78, example and scope.

## Evidence

**Basis:** Proposed uncertainty role-play with demonstration.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 138 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- The model is instructed to say it does not know, so the response says nothing about actual uncertainty.
- Its claim about what science needs to know can itself be wrong.
