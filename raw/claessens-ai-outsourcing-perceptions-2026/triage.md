## Summary

The authors study how people judge someone who delegates work to AI. Outsourcing affects perceived effort, authenticity, care and the value of the output. The pattern depends on both the task and how AI is used.

Socio-relational tasks, such as love letters and apologies, attract broader negative judgments than instrumental tasks. The conclusion distinguishes lower perceived competence or greater laziness from moral or trust judgments. These are observers' perceptions, not evidence of actual incompetence, dishonesty or relationship damage.
