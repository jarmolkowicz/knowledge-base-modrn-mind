## Summary
Wang and Zhou survey 434 university teachers about perceived AI replacement threat, burnout, organizational support, and organizational commitment. Greater AI threat awareness correlates with more burnout, while support and commitment correlate with less burnout and statistically mediate the relationship.

The paper suggests that communication, participation, training, recognition, and career development may buffer AI-related strain. Its cross-sectional design cannot show that AI awareness caused burnout or that the proposed support measures reduce it.
