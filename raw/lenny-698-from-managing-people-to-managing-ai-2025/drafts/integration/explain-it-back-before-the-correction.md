# Explain it back before the correction

## Use When

An AI explanation feels clear, but you want to see what you can actually explain yourself.

## Try It

1. Read or hear the explanation and then put it aside.
2. Explain the concept back in your own terms, example or analogy. Include where you are uncertain.
3. Ask AI to identify specific mismatches or missing steps rather than simply rate the explanation.
4. Check important corrections against the original material or a knowledgeable person. [Inference] Try a changed example later without the assistant to examine transfer rather than immediate repetition.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis / observed or reported:** First-person account of interactive study. Zhuo reports finding retelling useful and observes that politely worded feedback may mean the explanation was wrong. No independent learning measure.

**Related research:** [[fernandes-metacognition-2025]] proposes explain-back tasks but does not evaluate them. [[bjork-desirable-difficulties-2011]] supports examining retention and transfer separately from immediate fluent performance.

**Untested:** Improved understanding or calibration from this interaction. Agreement from the same model is not an independent test.

## Limits

AI can affirm a misconception or invent a correction. Generated rationales are not faithful records of a model’s internal reasoning. Use an accessible response form and keep human teaching available.

## What to Notice

[Inference] Can you explain the concept on a different example, and which parts still require help?
