# Fiction as a Mirror for Students Using AI

## Citation

Steve Peha, with Sam Illingworth. (2026-01-09). Fiction as a Mirror for Students Using AI. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/ai-fiction-student-reflection

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to use fiction to prompt independent reflective writing before group discussion.

## Key Findings

Peha reports personal reflection after a fictional-character prompt; no results from students using this exact classroom protocol.

**Locators:** L18–22, attribution; L30–40, classroom procedure; L60–106, personal example.

## Evidence

**Basis:** Proposed classroom exercise with adult first-person demonstration.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 184 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- A generated character voice is an interpretation, not the original work or the author’s intention.
- Private reflections need not be disclosed to the tool or classmates.
- Five minutes of writing does not establish long-term inquiry or learning gains.
