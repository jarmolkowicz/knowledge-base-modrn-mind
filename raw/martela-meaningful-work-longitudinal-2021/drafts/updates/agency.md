## Proposed Additions

### To "Key Insight"

Martela et al. (2021) offer a non-AI distinction: experienced autonomy and perceived positive contribution predicted later [[meaningful-work]] in the main three-month-lag models, while competence and relatedness did not show the same adjusted prospective pattern. This was a self-report cohort with heavy attrition, not an intervention. Felt autonomy is not a direct measure of decision authority or retained skill. The separate three-wave mediation hypothesis was not supported.
