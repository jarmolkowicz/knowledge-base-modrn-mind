## Summary
Across five experiments with 3,132 participants, Marcoccia and colleagues test whether access to AI advice changes willingness to suspend judgment on difficult questions. Four studies were preregistered and one directly replicated the first study.

AI access nearly eliminated "I don't know" responses even though the engineered questions usually produced wrong AI advice. Monetary consequences restored some judgment suspension, suggesting the effect is context-sensitive rather than an unavoidable cognitive limit.
