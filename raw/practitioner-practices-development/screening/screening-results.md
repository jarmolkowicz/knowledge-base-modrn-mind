## What this establishes

Expanded source passages were inspected, with targeted follow-up where useful. Most long articles and interviews were **not read in full**. This is completed queue screening, not completed source review or proof that any practice improves thinking. Practitioner credentials and cited research were not systematically verified.

The strongest additions make the person's contribution visible: write an answer first; explain a concept back; build the opposing argument; inspect actual evidence; choose among creative possibilities; retain human advisers. Examples include Marty Cagan changing his advice after observing overtrust, Julie Zhuo's teach-back routine, Seth Godin retaining authorship while asking for criticism, and Mike Krieger asking children how they could find out before consulting AI. Exact passages and limitations are in the [selected source notes](selected-source-notes.md).

The queue also adds counterexamples: rising AI grades, fabricated quotations, opaque agent work, and guesses about personality or feelings. Those are failure cases, not recommended prompts.
