## Proposed Additions

### To "Key Insight" section

Martela and Riekki (2018) associated felt autonomy, relatedness and beneficence with meaningful work across Finnish, Indian and US samples. Competence did not have a unique association in the US joint model. The cross-sectional surveys measured felt effectiveness and contribution, not retained skill or objective social impact. [Inference] An agency review can ask about these experiences, but should not substitute them for evidence of independent judgment or accountability.
