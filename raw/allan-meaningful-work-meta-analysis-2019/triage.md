## Summary

Allan and colleagues synthesize relationships between meaningful work and work attitudes, wellbeing and work-related outcomes. Associations with engagement, commitment and job satisfaction are stronger than those with some behavioral or performance measures.

The paper tests different statistical structures, but correlation-based model fit does not establish that meaningful work causes the outcomes. Some outcome groups contain fewer than five studies, and all included settings are Western.
