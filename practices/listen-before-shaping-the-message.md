# Listen before shaping the message

## Use When

You are about to ask an audience to change priorities or behavior.

## Try It

1. Hear their situation through interviews or surveys.
2. Compare what you hear with what the presentation will ask them to do.
3. Rehearse a rough version with relevant people before polishing slides.
4. Revise the message using their feedback, then deliver it.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Include affected people beyond the leadership layer where possible. Generated personas can suggest questions, not replace actual listening.
