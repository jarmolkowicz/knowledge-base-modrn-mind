# Answer AI questions with real evidence

Title is editorial, not a validated named method.

## Use When

Planning sounds clear while important facts remain missing or assumed.

## Try It

1. State the decision and separate facts from guesses.
2. Ask AI what missing information could change the choice. Return to actual records or responsible people; label what cannot be established.
3. Review options and decide yourself. Do not use model confidence percentages or cross-model agreement as the stopping rule.
