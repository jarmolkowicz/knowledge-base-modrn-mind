# Present projections with assumptions and alternatives

## Use When

People are using an AI-supported model or simulation to discuss an uncertain future. Someone can explain what the model assumes and what its output does not establish.

## Try It

1. State what the representation is being used to explore and what it cannot predict reliably.
2. Present important assumptions and limitations alongside the visual or projection.
3. Where the model supports it, compare scenarios or assumptions rather than presenting one image as an inevitable future.
4. Invite discussion of consequences, missing information and choices; keep the resulting decision distinct from the model output.

The authors propose scenario contrasts and narration of assumptions and limitations. [Inference] This ordered meeting routine is an editorial adaptation. Do not fabricate model-generated scenarios or numerical uncertainty ranges.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** Comparative ethnography of two urban-planning organizations using the same simulation technology, plus author recommendations. Selected original sections reviewed.

**Observed or reported:** The authors describe contrasting ways of framing representations and expert authority. They explicitly note a trade-off: heavy caveats or reduced detail may dampen stakeholder interest. They propose pairing engagement with visible uncertainty.

**Intended:** judgment, shared-understanding, calibration. These are curator-assigned intended benefits, not demonstrated effects.

**Untested:** The effectiveness of this exact routine in its proposed AI-use setting, including durable independent capability, better judgment and calibrated confidence. Immediate output quality, felt competence and later unaided performance are distinct outcomes.

## Limits

The study does not causally prove that this checklist improves decisions. Less detail is not always better; retaining expert authority is not itself evidence of sound judgment. AI-generated prose about uncertainty is not an uncertainty calculation.
