# Rehearse with someone who knows the audience

## Use When

An important presentation must withstand questions from particular people.

## Try It

1. Run the presentation with a colleague who knows those people.
2. Have them anticipate the questions and concerns each audience member may raise.
3. Revise the explanation and prepare the evidence needed.
4. Where appropriate, meet actual participants before the main meeting to hear concerns.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** After the real meeting, compare anticipated with actual questions. AI rehearsal may generate possibilities, not knowledge of those individuals.
