## Summary
Strömberg, Lei, and Wu analyze 30 months of panel data for 26,811 Chinese students in grades 7-12 using staggered AI adoption. AI use raises homework scores by 18% and reduces completion time by 30%, but monthly closed-book exam scores fall 20% within six months.

High-stakes entrance-exam scores fall 18-24%, with the full loss appearing after about two years. Losses concentrate among roughly 80% of AI users whose fast, high-scoring homework suggests outsourcing; users who maintain homework time show small losses.
