## Proposed Additions

### To "Key Insight" section

Niemiec, Ryan and Deci (2009) followed recent graduates across one year. Self-reported attainment of intrinsic aspirations was associated with better psychological health; extrinsic-attainment findings depended on outcome and adjustment for intrinsic attainment. The two-wave study did not establish causal direction. [Inference] Success at an AI-supported goal does not, by itself, show that the goal supports well-being, expresses the person's values or preserves their capability.
