# Record a lived moment each day

## Use When

You want material drawn from your experience rather than generated personal stories.

## Try It

1. Briefly note a moment that caught your attention, with its date.
2. Record during the day or reflect at its end; a few words are enough.
3. Mark older memories as memories so they do not appear to have happened that day.
4. Return to the notes and choose which, if any, are worth developing into stories.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Keep observations separate from interpretations of other people. AI may help search the archive later, but must not invent your experiences.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** First-person practitioner interview and advice.

**Observed or reported:** Reports collecting more moments over years and using some in stories.

**Intended:** Notice experience and build a personal story archive.

**Related research:** The cited account does not directly evaluate this routine; related entries provide context, not validation.

**Untested:** Causal effects on decision quality, retained unaided capability, confidence calibration and long-term use. A better artifact or a confident report cannot establish these.

## Limits

Self-reported frequency and coaching testimonials; no memory or therapeutic evaluation. Interpretations of others’ motives are not facts. Do not turn private experiences into public material without judgment.
