## Summary
Luettgau and colleagues ran a longitudinal randomized trial with a representative UK sample of 6,474 people. After one 20-minute conversation about health, careers, or relationships with one of three leading chatbots, up to 79% reported acting on the advice two to three weeks later.

Advice-following stayed above 60% for serious and hard-to-reverse recommendations, showing weak calibration to stakes. Conversations produced short-term mood benefits but no lasting well-being gain over casual chatbot conversation.
