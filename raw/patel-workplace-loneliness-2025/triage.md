## Summary
Patel, Peko, and Sundaram use literature, public data, and system-dynamics diagrams to model GenAI as both a cause of workplace loneliness and a possible companion or therapist. The paper combines job demands-resources and "bright ICT" ideas into proposed intervention artifacts.

This is the first design iteration: the models and proposed app have not been evaluated with employees, clinicians, or organizations. Claims that GenAI can address loneliness at its source therefore remain speculative.
