## Summary
Bainbridge explains why more automation can make the remaining human role harder rather than easier. Operators are left to monitor reliable systems and intervene only during unusual failures, precisely when they need skills, process knowledge, and situational understanding that routine automation prevents them from practicing.

The paper connects skill decay, weak monitoring, loss of current system context, and poor takeover performance. It argues for human-computer collaboration and system design that keeps operators meaningfully involved.
