# Practice choosing the questions that change the plan

## Use When

You generate many questions but struggle to identify which answers matter most.

## Try It

1. Choose a low-stakes fictional scenario, such as bringing a teleportation device to market.
2. Generate the questions you want answered.
3. Limit yourself to two and explain which later decisions each answer changes.
4. Compare possible answers and the different plans they imply.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Use a partner or AI to vary the scenario only after your attempt. Do not carry the artificial two-question limit into real safety or due-diligence work.
