## Proposed Additions

### Add under Factors Shaping Complementarity

Ali et al. (2025) map organizational context alongside user expertise and AI capability: task fit, explanation quality, resources, governance and room to challenge outputs. Their review is a framework synthesis, not a joint causal test. [Inference] When assessing a human–AI workflow, check these conditions alongside actual joint performance; adoption, trust and satisfaction alone do not demonstrate complementarity.
