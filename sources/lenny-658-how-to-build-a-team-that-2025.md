## Type and account basis

Hilary Gridley describes a GPT she built to generate LSAT-style reasoning questions in product-management contexts.

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to get more opportunities to reason about familiar work situations.

## Key Findings

The speaker shows the intended scenario/multiple-choice/feedback loop and says practice can be more frequent; no learning measurement.

## Open Questions and limits

- Generated answer keys can be wrong; product decisions are not always deductive with one right answer. More reps and subjective confidence do not establish transfer or calibration. Engineering estimates need actual system context.

## Related Practices

The related routine is proposed, not experimentally evaluated.

- [[rehearse-a-bounded-reasoning-case]]

**Application boundary [Inference]:** Use fact-bounded exercises and checked explanations; do not let simulated correctness settle a real product decision.
