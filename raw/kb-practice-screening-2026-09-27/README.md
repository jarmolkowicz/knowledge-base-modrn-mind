## How selection worked

1. Inventoried canonical source, concept and method entries. Used the prior practitioner integration record to avoid silently counting old work as a fresh review.
2. Looked for an identifiable action, sequence, tested intervention, author recommendation or existing KB adaptation relevant to thinking, judgment, creativity or intellectual agency. Keyword matches only located passages; they did not select or reject entries.
3. Traced related concepts and methods back to source notes. Several entries derived from one paper do not count as several independent pieces of evidence.
4. Compared the trigger and action with the existing practices. New evidence for an existing action went to enrichment; a potentially distinct routine went to the candidate list.
5. Kept research findings separate from prescriptions. A finding that AI sometimes impairs learning does not establish that any particular proposed safeguard works.

Evidence labels distinguish a studied package, research synthesis, observed practice, conceptual argument and editorial adaptation. Existing `solid` status is not sufficient evidence that a derived routine works.

## Next step

Take **all ten candidate families and eight enrichment routes** through source review, rather than treating a small subset as the whole opportunity. They can be processed in batches, with every family remaining on the register until resolved.

For each family: inspect the original source and any intervention materials; recover exact actions and locators; check population, task and measured outcomes; confirm the difference from existing practices; then decide **new draft / existing-practice variant or evidence update / hold / no distinct practice**. Record the reason. Drafts belong in the relevant `raw/<source>/drafts/` trail and require the normal critique and integration decision.

Do not extract a new card from each contributing KB entry. Keep methods as explanatory frameworks; any practice should link back to them and the original source.

The taxonomy stays provisional. This scan suggests areas worth testing against it—learning over time, recovery from automation failure, and organizational conditions for agency. It does not establish a final set of intended outcomes. Kindness and general wellbeing practices need a scope decision before inclusion.
