## Summary

The authors interpret meaningful work through temporality. Participants described meaningful episodes that connected present work with past practice, future contribution and other people; experiences of meaninglessness were linked to constrained or disrupted time.

This adds a temporal explanation rather than another general list of job attributes. Its qualitative accounts should not become a universal rule that meaning happens only after completion or that slower work necessarily has more value.
