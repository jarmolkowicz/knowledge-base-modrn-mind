# Sources selected for closer practice review

**Historical first-pass shortlist.** The 400-item queue has now been screened. Use the [consolidated shortlist](consolidated-shortlist.md); these 15 remain included there.

2026-09-26. Fifteen starting points selected after the whole-collection first pass. This is a reading shortlist, not a list of proven practices or approved KB entries. The wider queue remains open.

Selection rests on a visible procedure/example, attributable use or proposal, inspectable passages, and a distinct contribution. Practitioner credentials were not comprehensively verified. Claims of benefit remain separate from descriptions of use.

Headings describe source contributions, not an adopted outcome taxonomy. Line numbers refer to the saved original Markdown; timestamps to saved transcripts; EPUB markers identify documents, not printed chapters.

## Feedback without rewriting

**Rebecca J Hogue, hosted by Sam Illingworth** — [How Can Writers Use AI Ethically?](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Sam%20Illingworth/how-can-writers-use-ai-ethically.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_for_extraction`. **Locator:** L28-80.

Short writing sample; request a limited number of improvement areas; human makes the edits and can reject advice.

**Basis:** First-person use with a before/after example and explanation of the prompt constraint.

**Limits:** Reported learning is not measured unaided transfer. Separate the exercise from platform privacy claims.

**Read coverage:** Body and worked example inspected.

## Creative criticism with retained authorship

**Sam Illingworth; co-editor Alyssa Fu Ward; named creative contributors** — [How Creatives Are Actually Using AI (And What They Refuse to Let It Do)](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Sam%20Illingworth/how-creatives-actually-use-ai.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_for_extraction`. **Locator:** L61-84; L194-208.

Illingworth keeps a disputed poetic choice. Joshua Sherman describes revising his own lyrics after debate and building a checklist he uses without AI.

**Basis:** Named first-person accounts with concrete creative decisions; Sherman records as First Radio On.

**Limits:** Self-reports, not independent evaluations. Other contributors use different boundaries. Do not attribute every account to the editor or treat reuse as legal clearance.

**Read coverage:** Main body and contribution headings inspected; images not assessed.

## Test AI on the actual job

**Ethan Mollick** — [Giving your AI a Job Interview](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Ethan%20Mollick/giving-your-ai-a-job-interview.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_for_extraction`. **Locator:** L40-62.

Use realistic tasks, repeated attempts and expert assessment to learn where a model works for a particular job.

**Basis:** Worked model comparisons and research examples.

**Limits:** Model rankings age quickly. Better assisted output does not establish stronger human judgment or learning.

**Read coverage:** Expanded body and evaluation examples inspected.

## Build evaluation from observed errors

**Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar, interviewed by Lenny Rachitsky** — [Why AI evals are the hottest new skill for product builders  /  Hamel Husain & Shreya Shankar](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Lenny%27s%20Podcast/repo/episodes/hamel-husain-shreya-shankar/transcript.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_for_extraction`. **Locator:** 00:12:36-00:31:42; 00:56:28-00:57:18.

Inspect real traces, have a domain expert record failures, group errors, and compare automated judgments against human labels.

**Basis:** Detailed consulting/practitioner account using the Nurture Boss case; explicit example of missing domain knowledge.

**Limits:** Reliability of an AI system is the immediate outcome. Human capability growth remains unmeasured. ID653 repeats this transcript body.

**Read coverage:** Expanded timestamped passages; not the entire interview.

**Read coverage:** All six extracted PDF pages inspected.

## Question the assumptions in a belief or draft

**Laura O'Driscoll, hosted by Sam Illingworth** — [Use AI to Examine Your Beliefs and Reveal Hidden Assumptions](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Sam%20Illingworth/the-one-ai-prompt-to-examine-your-beliefs.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_with_flags`. **Locator:** L28-36; L56-80.

State a belief, invite clarifying questions, and examine assumptions; contributor also checks drafts for sweeping claims before publishing.

**Basis:** Concrete prompt plus first-person repeated use and an example question.

**Limits:** The model cannot reliably diagnose where a belief came from. Discomfort or reduced certainty is not evidence of improved accuracy; factual premises still need external checking.

**Read coverage:** Full available text inspected.

## Check claimed memories against external records

**J Hong, hosted by Sam Illingworth** — [Is your AI overwriting your memory?](../../../raw/inbox/Practical/Sam%20Illingworth/ai-overwriting-your-memory.md)

**Reading decision:** `read_as_counterexample`. **Locator:** L32-60.

A model claimed a dream and a source file existed; the contributor searched actual records. Preserve this as a failure case for prompt-only checking.

**Basis:** First-person failure account with a named checking action.

**Limits:** The advertised self-check prompt did not stop fabrication. No proof that a false memory was implanted; the underlying dream remains unresolved.

**Read coverage:** Entire available body inspected.
