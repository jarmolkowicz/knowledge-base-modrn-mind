## Summary

The authors ask whether a more balanced pattern of autonomy, competence and relatedness satisfaction is associated with wellbeing beyond the total level of satisfaction. They report a modest additional association across several designs.

The findings do not show an optimal numerical balance or that people should trade down one satisfied need to equalize scores. The authors call for experimental tests, broader samples and longer follow-up.
