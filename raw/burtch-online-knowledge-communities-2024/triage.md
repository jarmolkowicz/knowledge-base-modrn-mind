## Summary
Burtch, Lee, and Chen examine activity on Stack Overflow and Reddit developer communities around ChatGPT's release. Stack Overflow visits and question volumes declined, especially in topics where ChatGPT performed well, while Reddit developer communities did not show the same decline.

The decline was concentrated among newer users. The authors interpret the contrast as evidence that social fabric may buffer knowledge communities against substitution by AI, while noting that the short observational window and platform differences limit causal certainty and generalization.
