# Jia, Ning & Liu (2025)

## Citation

Jia, J., Ning, X., & Liu, W. (2025). The consequences and theoretical explanation of workplace AI on employees: A systematic literature review. Journal of Digital Management, 1, 14. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44362-025-00016-3

Published review; supplied publisher PDF, 23 pages.

Audit: [ingestion record](../raw/jia-workplace-ai-review-2025/log.md).

## Type

Systematic review and bibliometric/thematic synthesis of 107 articles, using Web of Science plus snowball searching.

## Key Insight

Workplace AI is not a single exposure: using an assistant, being managed by an algorithm, and fearing replacement are different conditions. They should not be merged into one claim about identity or well-being.

## Key Findings

- Separates organizational adoption and management from employees' usage, perceptions and abilities; measures differ across these constructs (pp.5–7).
- Organizes explanations through resource, stress, cognitive and motivational perspectives, with both beneficial and adverse pathways (pp.7–14).
- Identifies employee differences, work characteristics, organizational practices and leadership as boundary conditions in the reviewed literature (pp.14–16).
- Authors acknowledge that the four perspectives are not integrated into a unified theory, practical recommendations remain broad, and long-term effects need further study (p.18).

## Key Passages

> "we did not attempt to integrate them into a unified theoretical framework."
> — Jia, Ning & Liu (2025), PDF p.18

> "primarily fall into two categories: individual differences and working situations."
> — Jia, Ning & Liu (2025), PDF p.18; line wrapping normalized

## Relevance

Helps [[professional-identity-threat]] distinguish threat perceptions, work arrangements and measured outcomes.

## Supports

- [[professional-identity-threat]] — outcomes vary with the kind of AI exposure and workplace context.

## Contradicts / Extends

Counters a uniform decline narrative by retaining resource gains and more positive appraisals alongside threats. Does not supply a pooled effect or an independently tested causal chain.

## Limitations

- Main database search limited to English-language articles in selected Web of Science subject areas, supplemented by snowball searching.
- Different technologies, measures and designs are synthesized; not all findings concern generative AI.
- No pooled effect, formal comparison of effect strength across theories, or direct intervention test of the combined framework.
- Individual primary studies were not independently re-audited here; their results are not counted as new replications.
- Long-term career and capability changes remain research questions.

## Open Questions

- Which implementation choices preserve control and meaning while reducing burdens?
- How do outcomes change with sustained exposure rather than initial adoption?
