## Summary

The study distinguishes wanting goals from attaining them. Intrinsic aspiration attainment was associated with psychological need satisfaction and better psychological health; extrinsic attainment did not show the same pattern.

Change in attainment and change in health were correlated. The authors explicitly state that the design does not establish direction of effects. Neither the paper's title nor its mediation analysis justifies saying that pursuing money causes poor wellbeing.
