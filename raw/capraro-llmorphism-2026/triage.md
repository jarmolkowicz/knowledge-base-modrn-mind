## Summary
Capraro defines LLMorphism as the biased belief that human cognition works like a large language model. Fluent machine language may encourage a reverse inference: because LLMs speak like humans, people may begin to describe humans as prediction systems that generate outputs from inputs.

The paper proposes analogical transfer and metaphorical availability as mechanisms. Possible consequences include confusing fluency with expertise, thinning agency and responsibility, ignoring embodiment and affect, and making people appear more replaceable.
