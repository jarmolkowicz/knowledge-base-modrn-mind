# What Claude Cannot Read in a Poem

## Citation

Sam Illingworth. What Claude Cannot Read in a Poem. 2026-03-21. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/what-claude-cannot-read-in-a-poem

## Type and account basis

Sam Illingworth reports an open exploratory exercise: one model, ten poems, thirty outputs and one human coder.

## Key Insight

The article examines limits of AI as a critical reader.

## Key Findings

The author reports competent formal analysis, a reading that changed his view of one poem, and differences from his personal associations.
