# Check missing perspectives against real evidence

## Use When

An AI summary treats a varied group as if everyone had the same circumstances.

## Try It

1. Read the answer and name a person or situation it may overlook.
2. Ask AI who might be disadvantaged or missing; treat its suggestions as leads.
3. Consult an actual source, lived account or affected person.
4. Revise only where that evidence warrants it.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Keep an omission that is justified by scope distinct from one that distorts the answer.
