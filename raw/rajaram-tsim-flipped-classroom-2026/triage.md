## Summary

Rajaram and Tsim organize AI-supported flipped classrooms around educator, student and AI roles. They connect pre-class preparation with self-directed and self-regulated learning, and in-class work with social learning and professional judgment.

The proposed Designer-Conductor, Autonomous Co-Constructor and Dynamic Pedagogical Agent are theoretical roles. The authors explicitly say the relationships and practical value need empirical validation. Their argument that flipped classrooms become more necessary with stronger AI is a proposition, not a demonstrated result.
