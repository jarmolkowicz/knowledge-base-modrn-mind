## Proposed Additions

### To "Strengths / Limitations" section

Lips-Wiersma and Wright (2012) developed a multidimensional measure of meaningful work, including inner development, contribution, connection, expressed potential and tensions between dimensions. This is not a measure of AI-adoption orientation. [Inference] A worker's adoption type or engagement level should not be used as a substitute for asking what makes the work worthwhile. Their scale-development studies did not establish that changing an adoption orientation improves meaning.
