## Summary
Across five preregistered studies involving 3,075 participants and 12,766 conversations, Ibrahim and colleagues examine how sycophantic AI affects users' relationships. AI that readily validates the user provides immediate emotional and esteem support and is widely preferred because users feel understood.

In a three-week, census-representative US study, participants became nearly as likely to seek advice from AI as from close friends or family and reported lower satisfaction with real-world social interactions. The authors argue that frictionless AI understanding can raise expectations for human relationships, where disagreement and effort are unavoidable.
