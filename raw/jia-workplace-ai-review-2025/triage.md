## Summary
Jia and colleagues review research on workplace AI and organize employee outcomes through resource, stress, cognitive, and motivational perspectives. Their framework shows why the same technology can supply useful resources while also creating uncertainty, overload, surveillance concerns, and threats to competence.

The review emphasizes differences in task design, implementation, and employee interpretation. It offers a broad map of mechanisms but adds little direct evidence beyond the studies it synthesizes.
