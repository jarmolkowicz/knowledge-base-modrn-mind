### To "Key Insight" in basic-psychological-needs

Sheldon and Niemiec (2006) examined the profile of autonomy, felt competence and relatedness alongside their levels across four observational studies. More balanced profiles showed modest additional associations with adjustment. This was not uniform: in Study 1 the subjective well-being association was nonsignificant after squared need terms were included (PDF p.4). The findings do not establish that everyone needs equal scores, that improving one need harms people when others stay unchanged, or that deliberately equalizing scores improves well-being. Felt competence remains distinct from tested ability, and need ratings are not a measure of accountable control.
