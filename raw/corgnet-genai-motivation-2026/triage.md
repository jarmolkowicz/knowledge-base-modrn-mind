## Summary
Corgnet, Fumagalli, and Rezaei compare collaboration with a human coworker and with GenAI under matched task conditions. In a preregistered experiment with 1,091 participants, human-AI teams performed better than human-human teams.

Contrary to common expectations, the AI condition also increased perceived relatedness, competence, autonomy, job satisfaction, and interest. The findings challenge claims that AI collaboration inherently undermines intrinsic motivation and create a useful contrast with studies reporting alienation or motivational loss.
