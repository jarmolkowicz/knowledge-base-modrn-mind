# Explain the other side before deciding

## Use When

A team is stuck between competing priorities.

## Try It

1. Ask each side for the strongest reasons supporting the other option.
2. Check that the opposing side recognizes its position in that account.
3. Clarify the decision maker and deadline.
4. Decide after the discussion and make the rationale visible.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** The recognition check and written rationale are curator additions. An AI role-play can prepare you, but does not replace hearing the actual parties.
