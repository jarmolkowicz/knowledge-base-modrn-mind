## Main changes

1. **Replace broad claims where needed.** Adding cautious paragraphs below unconditional headlines would leave conflicting advice. Revise the proposed edits to agency, partial-automation-principle and ai-loneliness-effect. Merge related proposals together.
2. **Separate the two new identity concepts.** Competence authenticity concerns perceived alignment between results and personal mastery. AI impostor feelings concern a fraudulence response after success. Add the distinction and cross-links; the studies have not tested their empirical independence.
3. **Preserve measurement limits in short entries.** High advice-following does not alone show poor judgment. A survey association is not a randomized effect. Werner's concept must retain the prior seller-interest warning and nonrandomized awareness caveat.
4. **Finish citation/version cleanup.** Capraro and Marcoccia now have matching preprint records. Jowsey and Zhu require 2025 canonical references; Rathje needs supplied-version confirmation against a matching 2025 record.
5. **Keep Zhang out of positive evidence claims.** Printed outcome/interaction labels remain unclear. A sign reversal alone is not proof of error or misconduct; the combined reporting problems warrant clarification.
