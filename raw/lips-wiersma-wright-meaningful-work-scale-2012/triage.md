## Summary

The paper develops a multidimensional measure of meaningful work. It includes developing the inner self, unity with others, serving others and expressing full potential, together with tensions between self/others and being/doing, and inspiration/reality.

The authors distinguish meaningfulness from its proposed antecedents and outcomes. Scale development provides measurement evidence, not a demonstration that improving a score or changing one dimension causes better performance or preserves skills.
