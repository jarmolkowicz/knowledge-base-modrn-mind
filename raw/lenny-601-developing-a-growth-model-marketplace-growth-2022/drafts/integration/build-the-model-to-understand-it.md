# Build the explanatory model yourself

## Use When

A ready-made model gives outputs you cannot yet explain.

## Try It

1. Start with a small conceptual model of the system.
2. Build the relevant team-level drivers with an analyst or knowledgeable partner.
3. Make the assumptions and trade-offs explicit.
4. Compare model expectations with actual results and revise over time.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** AI can assist calculations or ask questions after you form the relationships; check its work. This AI use is a transfer hypothesis.
