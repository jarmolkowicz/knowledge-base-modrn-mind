## Summary
Frenzel and Piasentin use industry-focused groups and self-determination theory to examine why employees avoid AI tools. Participants describe AI as either supporting or undermining autonomy, competence, and relatedness depending on how it is introduced and used.

The paper proposes "competence authenticity": employees may perform well with AI while feeling that the result no longer demonstrates their own mastery. This helps explain avoidance that is not reducible to low digital skill or generic resistance to change.
