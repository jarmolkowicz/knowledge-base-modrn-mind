## Summary

The paper links job characteristics, experienced psychological states, individual growth needs and work outcomes. It provides an original empirical foundation for discussing how autonomy, feedback, skill variety, task identity and task significance relate to motivation.

Support is not uniform across outcomes. The discussion notes weaker results for performance and absence and possible common-method inflation for attitudinal measures. This is not an AI study or a randomized demonstration that redesigning any job will improve all outcomes.

## Quality

- Evidence basis: Job Characteristics Model tested with 658 employees in 62 jobs across seven organizations.
