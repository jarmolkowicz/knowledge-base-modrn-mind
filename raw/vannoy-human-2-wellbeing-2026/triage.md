## Summary
Vannoy, Cadieux, and Lyubomirsky review AI research on well-being interventions, learning, interpersonal skills, mediated communication, companionship, and personal growth. Short-term benefits appear possible, but depend on structure, feedback, use pattern, and context.

The review argues that AI often delivers an established intervention rather than producing the benefit itself. Structured use that supports human effort looks more promising than unstructured relational use that replaces human capacities or connection.
