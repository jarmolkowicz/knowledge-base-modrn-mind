## Summary
Hemenway Falk and Tsoukalas model a demand externality in firm-level automation decisions. A firm captures the private cost savings from automating tasks but does not bear the full demand loss created when displaced workers have less income to spend.

The model predicts socially excessive automation and proposes a Pigouvian automation tax as a correction. Its main contribution concerns labor economics and aggregate demand rather than human cognition, identity, or agency in AI use.
