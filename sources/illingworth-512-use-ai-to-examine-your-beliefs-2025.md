# Use AI to Examine Your Beliefs and Reveal Hidden Assumptions

## Citation

Laura O'Driscoll, with Sam Illingworth. (2025-12-09). Use AI to Examine Your Beliefs and Reveal Hidden Assumptions. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/the-one-ai-prompt-to-examine-your-beliefs

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to examine assumptions and the reasons for holding a belief.

## Key Findings

O’Driscoll describes questions that paused her thinking and says some positions changed; no independent assessment.

**Locators:** L28–36, Step-by-step; L56–80, A moment from Laura; L100–108, What to do with it.

## Evidence

**Basis:** First-person repeated-use account and proposed prompt.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 186 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- Questions can embed a false premise about upbringing, motives or emotion.
- Felt insight is not evidence that a revised belief is true.
