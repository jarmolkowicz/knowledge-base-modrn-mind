# Burtch, Lee & Chen (2024) — Online Knowledge Communities

## Citation

Burtch, G., Lee, D., & Chen, Z. (2024). The consequences of generative AI for online knowledge communities. *Scientific Reports, 14*, 10413. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-61221-0

## Type

Paper — peer-reviewed quasi-experimental analysis of website traffic and public posting behavior.

## Key Insight

ChatGPT's release was followed by less traffic and question posting on Stack Overflow, especially from newer accounts. Comparable Reddit developer communities showed no detectable posting decline. The contrast suggests that peer relationships may sustain a community when AI can supply answers, but the study does not isolate social connection as the causal mechanism.

## Evidence

- A synthetic control using LASSO compared daily Stack Overflow traffic with a weighted combination of other websites, drawing on SimilarWeb's top-1,000-site dataset from September 2022 to March 2023. The estimated decline was about one million daily visitors, roughly 12% of traffic immediately before release (p. 2).
- Difference-in-differences models compared posting around the November 30, 2022 release with the same calendar window a year earlier. Analysis covered the 50 most popular Stack Overflow tags and corresponding Reddit communities where available (pp. 2–4).
- Stack Overflow question volumes fell; Reddit posting did not show detectable declines. Larger topic-level declines roughly aligned with proxies for publicly available training material (pp. 3–5).
- The average age of posting accounts rose, as did the frequency of words with six or more characters in questions. These support an interpretation of reduced participation by newer accounts and a changed question mix, rather than directly measuring expertise or question difficulty (pp. 4–6).

## Relevance

Adds a community-level concern to [[novice-vulnerability]]: getting an answer privately can reduce occasions to meet and learn from peers. Lost mentoring and workplace learning are implications to investigate, not outcomes this study measured.

## Supports

- [[novice-vulnerability]] — reduced participation by newer accounts may change access to peer learning; account tenure is not a direct skill measure.

## Contradicts / Extends

- Extends the KB's mainly individual account of learning risks to shared knowledge production.
- Community displacement and a social-fabric buffer describe findings and hypotheses, not independently validated constructs.

## Open Questions

- Causal interpretation assumes no coincident events explain the change; individual ChatGPT adoption was not observed.
- Platform differences could explain the Reddit contrast; social ties were not directly manipulated or measured for each user.
- The observation window is only several months and software-focused. Effects on workplace learning, other domains, and long-term participation remain unknown.
- Evidence summarized here is limited to the eight-page main paper; the separate supplement is not included.
