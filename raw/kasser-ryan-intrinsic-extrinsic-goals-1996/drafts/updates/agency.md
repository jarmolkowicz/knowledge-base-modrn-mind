## Proposed Additions

### To "Key Insight" section

Goal content and goal ownership are different. Kasser and Ryan (1996) associated relative emphasis on intrinsic rather than extrinsic aspirations with well-being, but distinguished what a person pursues from autonomous or controlled reasons for pursuing it. The studies were correlational; they do not show that external rewards always undermine agency. [Inference] When reviewing AI-supported goals, ask separately whether the goal matters to the person and whether they endorse how it was chosen.
