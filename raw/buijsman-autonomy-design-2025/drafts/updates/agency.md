## Proposed Additions

### To "Key Insight" section

Buijsman, Carter and Bermúdez (2025) distinguish two requirements of domain-specific autonomy: the competence to make informed decisions and the ability to recognize and endorse the values guiding them. AI may undermine the first through missing failure signals or reduced practice, and the second through unnoticed value shifts. Their philosophical account also makes an important qualification: giving up a lower-level skill can support wider goals. The concern is whether the person loses capabilities needed to assess decisions or ownership of the values behind them, not whether every task remains unaided. Longer-term value changes and several proposed safeguards still need testing.
