## Summary
Kahneman and Klein reconcile two traditions: research on expert intuition and research on judgment biases. They argue that skilled intuition can be valid, but only when the environment contains sufficiently stable cues and the person has prolonged opportunities to learn those cues through practice and feedback.

Subjective confidence is not reliable evidence of expertise. Confidence may reflect a coherent story even when the environment is low-validity and provides no basis for accurate intuition.
