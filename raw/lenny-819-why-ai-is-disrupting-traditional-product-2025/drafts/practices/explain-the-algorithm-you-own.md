# Explain the algorithm you are responsible for

## Use When

You own a product whose algorithm shapes people’s choices.

## Try It

1. Write down the objective the algorithm is meant to optimize.
2. Identify its inputs and the data collection or tuning choices that matter.
3. Discuss the description with the people implementing it and resolve what you do not know.
4. Use that understanding in product review and investment decisions.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Record intended objective separately from observed behavior and test cases. For third-party tools, record unknowns instead of inventing internals.
