# Three-lens practice critique

## Evidence lens

Author reports an emotionally meaningful conversation. This supports a bounded account of what the source reports, not causal effectiveness.

## Practitioner lens

Asks a model to infer what the user is not saying; interprets a responsive exchange as shared understanding. The original framing is unsuitable as a recommended routine.

## Adversarial lens

A meaningful experience does not establish access to hidden motives. Creative reader-response hypotheses would require reframing and external checks, not adoption of the original mind-reading premise.
