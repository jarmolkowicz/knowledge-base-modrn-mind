# Make AI disclosure specific and reciprocal

## Use When

You use AI in work judged by others or write a policy governing their use.

## Try It

1. Name the tools, the tasks they assisted and the tasks you performed.
2. State what you checked and who owns the final judgments and errors.
3. Make that statement available to the affected audience.
4. Apply the same expectations to policy authors; where appropriate, involve affected people in writing the policy.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Use safe representative channels where challenging a policy carries a power risk; review the statement when the workflow changes.
