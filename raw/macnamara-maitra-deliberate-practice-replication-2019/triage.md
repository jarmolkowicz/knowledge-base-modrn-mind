## Summary
Macnamara and Maitra conduct a preregistered, double-blind replication of Ericsson et al.'s violin study. They reproduce the broad method while improving blinding and analysis and distinguishing practice alone from teacher-designed practice.

They find a substantial relationship between practice and performance, but do not replicate the original claim that accumulated practice cleanly separates every skill level. Teacher-designed practice does not explain more performance variance than practice alone. The paper narrows, rather than dismisses, the value of deliberate practice.
