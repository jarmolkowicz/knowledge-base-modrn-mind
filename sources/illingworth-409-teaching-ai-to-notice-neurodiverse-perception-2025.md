# Teaching AI to Notice Neurodiverse Perception

## Citation

Susan Gordon Byron, with Sam Illingworth. (2025-11-27). Teaching AI to Notice Neurodiverse Perception. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/ai-and-neurodiverse-perception

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to notice whether a tool respects a stated sensory association rather than correcting it as a factual error.

## Key Findings

Byron reports questioning Lumo about sensory and mathematical transformations; no accessibility evaluation.

**Locators:** L16, attribution; L36–52, sensory prompt; L72–82, Lumo account.

## Evidence

**Basis:** Proposed exercise and first-person exploration.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 176 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- The model has no demonstrated felt association; its explanation is generated language.
- One response cannot diagnose a neurotypical system or represent all neurodiverse users.
- Respecting a creative association differs from agreeing with a harmful factual belief.
