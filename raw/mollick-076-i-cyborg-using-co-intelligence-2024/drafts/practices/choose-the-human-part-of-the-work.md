# Choose the human part of the work

Draft only. Integration pending. Title is editorial, not a validated named method.

## Use When

Some parts of an assisted task matter for your judgment, voice or learning.

## Try It

1. [Inference] Name the contribution you want to make yourself and why it matters.
2. Use AI for a bounded complementary part: alternatives, critique or a technical subtask.
3. Accept or reject specific contributions and complete the human part yourself. Mollick describes keeping his wording, reading the work and rejecting some criticism.
