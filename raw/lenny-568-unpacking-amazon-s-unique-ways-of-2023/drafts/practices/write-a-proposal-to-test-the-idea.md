# Write a proposal to test the idea

## Use When

A product idea sounds plausible but its customer, problem or solution is still vague.

## Try It

1. Write a plain customer/problem/solution statement and the important questions it raises.
2. Use evidence to make the customer and problem specific.
3. Review first with a small group, then wider groups where the idea warrants it.
4. Revise, compare alternatives or abandon the idea when the writing and critique expose weaknesses.
