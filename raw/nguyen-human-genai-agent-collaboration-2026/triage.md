## Summary
Nguyen and colleagues survey 310 employed GenAI users and model links among perceived AI value, privacy and vulnerability concerns, self-concern, collaboration, knowledge sharing, thriving, and well-being. Perceived value strongly predicts reported collaboration; collaboration correlates with all three positive outcomes.

Self-concern also has a small positive association with collaboration, while privacy and vulnerability concerns do not. Because all variables come from one cross-sectional self-report survey, the study cannot establish that collaboration caused thriving or well-being.
