# What should these practices improve?

Historical first proposal, 2026-09-26. Superseded for current review by the [2026-09-27 outcome proposal and complete mapping](outcome-review.md). Preserved to show how the categories changed.

Provisional, derived from the reviewed pool. **Do not turn the 19 contribution groups into outcome labels.** Those groups mix actions, contexts and failure cases. “Ask better questions” describes something we do; “make a better-founded decision” describes a possible result.

The table below is an editorial proposal, not a validated taxonomy. One practice can serve several outcomes. None of these benefits follows simply because the person stayed involved or the output improved.

## Keep four distinctions in every card

1. **Intended benefit:** what the author hopes the exercise will do.
2. **Reported result:** what actually happened in the available account, including subjective experience.
3. **Evaluated outcome:** what was compared or measured, if anything. Most cards here do not have a direct evaluation.
4. **Observation to try:** a curator-proposed check that could make personal use informative. This is not a validated measure or a causal experiment.

An improvement in one dimension can cost another. Generating alternatives may widen options while anchoring the final choice. Rehearsal may improve delivery while hiding weak evidence. Disclosure may improve inspectability while attracting unfair judgment. A smoother workflow may reduce the practice needed for later independent performance.

## What should remain outside the outcome labels

- **Procedure:** draft first, ask for objections, check a source, explain back, rehearse, keep a log.
- **Context:** writing, learning, product decisions, management, personal reflection.
- **Assistance pattern:** no AI, AI after a human attempt, alternating work, delegated execution with review.
- **Basis:** proposed exercise, demonstration, first-person account, reported field use, direct evaluation.
- **Source status:** usable with limits, supporting variant, counterexample, deferred.

Keeping these separate lets us ask whether two different procedures pursue the same outcome, or one procedure serves different outcomes in different contexts. It also stops practitioner reputation from becoming an outcome score.

## Gaps to test against the wider queue

The selected pool is rich in writing, education, product work and software. It is thinner on everyday civic judgment, non-digital craft, collective power, accessibility and long-term use outside professional knowledge work. These are gaps in this collection, not claims that no practices exist.

The 92 screened variants can later test whether the proposed distinctions hold or merely reflect the selected authors. Access and provenance holds should be resolved when they contain a specific missing contribution. Further reading should be able to change the categories; do not force new material into them.
