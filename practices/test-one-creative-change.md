# Test one creative change suggested by AI

## Use When

A draft feels incomplete but you do not want to rewrite the whole project.

Author’s intended benefit: Use generated material as a structural provocation for a bounded human experiment.

## Try It

1. Supply a short section or outline and ask AI to propose a missing chapter connecting its ideas.
2. Read it as a possible direction, not a discovery of your hidden intention.
3. Choose one small change: move a paragraph, rewrite a passage in a consistent voice, or try another point of view.
4. Test that change for 30 minutes, the source’s suggested timebox, and decide whether it serves the piece.
5. A variant from Harry O’Hanley (530) uses imagined earlier-era constraints to suggest a change. [Inference] Verify historical claims before using them as evidence.

### Earlier-era constraint variant

O’Hanley (530) proposes asking for current, 1990 and 1970 perspectives, each limited to 150 words and containing one insight, one blind spot and one possible decision. Choose one useful simplification and test it for 30 minutes. [Inference] These are imagined constraints unless checked against period sources; the prompt cannot guarantee historically bounded knowledge.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** Proposed creative procedure; reflection rather than documented before/after project.

**Observed or reported:** No finished project comparison in this source; later reader reports appear in source 530.

**Related research:** Doshi and Hauser’s short-story experiment separates ratings of individual outputs from diversity across outputs. It does not evaluate this practice or establish lasting creative skill. See [[doshi-hauser-creativity-diversity-2024]]. This is context, not direct validation.

**Untested:** Whether this specific procedure improves judgment, independent capability, creative quality or calibration beyond the reported experience. A completed artifact, more options and felt clarity are different outcomes.

## Limits

- AI cannot discover the uniquely correct missing chapter or read the writer’s unspoken intention.
- Generated autobiographical detail is not memory.
- Thirty minutes is a suggested timebox, not an evaluated dose.

## What to Notice

[Inference] Compare the changed passage with the saved original and explain what improved or was lost. You may reject the suggestion.
