# Wang & Zhou (2025) — AI Replacement Concerns and Burnout

## Citation

Wang, D., & Zhou, X. (2025). The impact of AI awareness on employees’ job burnout: A chain mediation of perceived organizational support and organizational commitment. SAGE Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/21582440251400532

Supplied 12-page published article, October–December 2025. Page locators refer to the supplied PDF.

[Retained source text](../raw/wang-ai-awareness-burnout-2025/source.md).

## Type

Cross-sectional convenience survey of 434 full-time university faculty in China, collected September–December 2024. No intervention or longitudinal follow-up.

## Key Insight

Concern about AI replacing one's role was associated with higher self-reported burnout. Organizational support and commitment were associated with lower burnout, but the study does not show that support interventions reduce AI-related strain.

## Key Findings

- Of 452 submitted questionnaires, 434 were retained. Recruitment used faculty leaders and WeChat groups; this is not a representative workforce sample (p.4).
- The AI-awareness scale measures replacement concerns, not AI literacy, actual use or an objective amount of automation (p.5).
- Regression and sequential mediation models link replacement concerns, support, commitment and burnout. Statistical indirect paths in same-time survey data do not establish a temporal mechanism (pp.6–8).
- Suggested communication, participation, training and recognition programs were not tested (p.9).
- The authors acknowledge self-report bias, a single cultural/occupational setting and inability to infer causality (pp.9–10).

## Limitations

Cross-sectional self-report, convenience sample, adapted scales with item removal, gender imbalance and no independent AI-use measure. Signs and formatting in the available tables are ambiguous, so exact regression, mediation and correlation estimates remain uncertain. The directional findings above follow the article's repeated prose account.

## Open Questions

- Do support changes precede lower burnout in longitudinal or randomized studies?
- Do observed relationships hold outside Chinese university faculty?
