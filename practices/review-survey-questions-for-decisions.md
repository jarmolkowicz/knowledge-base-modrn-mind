# Review survey questions against the decision

## Use When

A survey must yield interpretable answers rather than a number that looks useful.

## Try It

1. Write the decision the answers should inform.
2. Ask a survey specialist or AI to critique each item, including bundled questions.
3. Split ambiguous items and check what each possible answer would mean.
4. Remove items that cannot inform an action or answer a relevant question.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Try revised wording with a real respondent; AI approval is only a suggestion.

## Evidence and Rationale

**Basis:** First-person practitioner interview and advice.

**Observed or reported:** Explains why a build-and-test/slow-or-complicated item is ambiguous.

**Intended:** Make survey responses interpretable.

**Related research:** The cited account does not directly evaluate this routine; related entries provide context, not validation.

**Untested:** Causal effects on decision quality, retained unaided capability, confidence calibration and long-term use. A better artifact or a confident report cannot establish these.

## Limits

Expert advice and worked example, not a test of AI survey design. A model calling an item good is not validation. Productivity attribution remains confounded by concurrent changes.
