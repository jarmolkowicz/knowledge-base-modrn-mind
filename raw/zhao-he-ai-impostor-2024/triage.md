## Summary
Zhao and He report three experiments and one multiwave survey involving 734 US and Chinese employees. AI-augmented work increases impostor thoughts: people struggle to internalize success when the result depends on capabilities they do not feel they own.

The effect is stronger among highly intrinsically motivated workers. In the multiwave study, impostor thoughts link AI augmentation with lower knowledge sharing and interpersonal helping.
