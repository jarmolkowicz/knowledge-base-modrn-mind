## Summary

The authors report that meaningful moments can be personal, episodic, reflective, connected to others and emotionally difficult rather than simply pleasant. Interviewees described meaninglessness in relation to unfair treatment, pointless tasks, ignored judgment, isolation and value conflict.

The article proposes organizational, job, task and interactional conditions that may support meaningfulness. These are interpretations and practical suggestions drawn from interviews, not experimentally validated management practices. Meaning is not something managers can simply impose.
