# Ask advisers who know the subject and you

## Use When

Generic advice leaves you uncertain about a real choice.

## Try It

1. Form a specific question and ask for reasons, keeping the request light.
2. Choose some people with subject expertise and some who know your circumstances.
3. Look for surprises, disagreement and missing context across replies.
4. Make your own decision, thank contributors and report back on the result.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Use neutral wording rather than asking others to approve your preferred plan. AI may help edit the question, but cannot supply those people’s actual judgments.
