## Summary
Haddadin studies how working professionals experience AI's effects on power, trust, engagement, and collaboration. The thesis combines eight semi-structured interviews with a survey of 40 professionals.

Participants report both facilitation and disruption: faster work and broader access to knowledge alongside uncertainty, weaker trust, and mixed effects on engagement. Transparent implementation and human oversight are presented as conditions for better outcomes.
