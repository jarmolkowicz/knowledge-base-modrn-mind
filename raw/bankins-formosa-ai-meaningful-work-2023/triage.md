## Summary

The paper asks how AI deployment changes the meaningfulness of work that remains for people. It compares replacing tasks, tending the machine and amplifying human skills across task integrity, skill cultivation/use, task significance, autonomy and belongingness.

The authors describe possible benefits and harms rather than a uniform automation effect. Their proposed relationships and ethical guidance require empirical testing. Job design and distribution of benefits matter alongside technical capability.
