# Record a workflow before turning it into instructions

## Use When

A recurring task has tacit steps or exceptions worth documenting.

## Try It

1. Walk through actual work and capture notes or an appropriate recording, avoiding unnecessary confidential material.
2. Ask AI to clarify scope, inputs, quality, exceptions, prohibited actions, approvals and escalation before drafting instructions.
3. [Inference] Compare the draft with another walkthrough including an exception; correct omissions and make human decisions explicit.

## Limits

One example is not the workflow. Clean instructions can hide judgment or dignify a poor process.
