# Ask how workplace disclosures will be used

## Use When

An onboarding or workplace assistant asks for personal or contextual information.

## Try It

1. Ask what additional data is collected beyond your answer.
2. Ask who can access it, whether it leaves the organization and how it is used in assessment.
3. Consider whether knowing the destination would change your answer.
4. Seek answers from an accountable owner or actual policy before making the disclosure decision.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** The final verification step narrows the source’s advice: a generated reply cannot certify a vendor’s actual practices.
