## Summary
Ali and colleagues review 101 articles on human-AI collaboration in knowledge ecosystems. They organize findings into antecedents such as trust and expertise, mediators such as cognitive alignment and explanation, moderators such as task complexity and ethics, and outcomes such as decision quality and satisfaction.

The review also identifies power imbalance, opacity, passive reliance, and deskilling. It offers a broad organizational map, but its claims mostly consolidate familiar human-AI collaboration factors rather than testing new mechanisms.
