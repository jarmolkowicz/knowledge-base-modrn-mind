# Practices by intended outcome

**83 integrated practices.** Choose a situation or outcome, then open the practice for steps, source accounts, limits and things to observe.

These routines come from practitioner accounts, research, proposed exercises and documented examples. Inclusion is not proof of effectiveness or a blanket endorsement of an author. Research-based adaptations, AI adaptations and curator observations are labeled. Each entry distinguishes intended benefits from reported results.

The outcome vocabulary is provisional. Core outcomes concern human thinking and participation; enabling outcomes support them through accountability or immediate work quality. Better assisted output does not by itself show improved judgment, retained skill or well-calibrated confidence.

<a id="understanding"></a>

## Understanding

Explain a relationship, mechanism or limit and apply it to a case.

Not recognition of fluent prose or an AI rating.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Ask for an explanation in a specific case](practices/ask-for-an-explanation-in-a-specific-case.md) | An educator wants to see whether learners can apply a concept, beyond recalling a polished description. |
| [Build the explanatory model yourself](practices/build-the-model-to-understand-it.md) | A ready-made model gives outputs you cannot yet explain. |
| [Check an analogy’s relationships and limits](practices/check-an-analogys-relationships-and-limits.md) | An analogy makes an unfamiliar situation feel understandable or suggests a decision. You have enough knowledge of both situations to check the comparison. |
| [Explain it back before the correction](practices/explain-it-back-before-the-correction.md) | An AI explanation feels clear, but you want to see what you can actually explain yourself. |
| [Explain the algorithm you are responsible for](practices/explain-the-algorithm-you-own.md) | You own a product whose algorithm shapes people’s choices. |
| [Record a workflow before turning it into instructions](practices/record-a-workflow-before-turning-it-into-instructions.md) | A recurring task has tacit steps or exceptions worth documenting. |
| [Teach a simulated learner](practices/teach-a-simulated-learner.md) | You know a framework and want to practise applying it through explanation, rather than ask AI to explain it again. |
| [Write a proposal to test the idea](practices/write-a-proposal-to-test-the-idea.md) | A product idea sounds plausible but its customer, problem or solution is still vague. |

<a id="independent-capability"></a>

## Independent capability

Perform a chosen skill under explicitly reduced-assistance conditions, including later use.

An immediate attempt is practice or assessment, not evidence of durable transfer. Keep accommodations.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Ask about the gap, then do the task](practices/ask-about-the-gap-then-do-the-task.md) | You are learning a task that you would otherwise hand to AI, and want the attempt itself to remain yours. |
| [Ask how to find out before asking AI](practices/ask-how-to-find-out-before-asking-ai.md) | A child asks a factual question and reaching for AI would end the inquiry immediately. |
| [Build the opposing case yourself](practices/build-the-opposing-case-yourself.md) | A classroom or learning group wants students to practise reasoning rather than watch AI conduct both sides of a debate. |
| [Check a capability without the assistant](practices/check-a-capability-without-the-assistant.md) | You need to retain a specific capability even when the usual AI tool is unavailable. The goal is a bounded skill, not proving general independence from support. |
| [Let the child write the story around supporting images](practices/let-the-child-write-the-story.md) | A child wants to make a story and the chosen activity is writing, while images can support it. |
| [Mix problem types before choosing a solution](practices/mix-problem-types-before-choosing-a-solution.md) | You can attempt several related problem types but need practice recognizing which approach fits. Begin with suitable instruction and checked examples. |
| [Practice choosing the questions that change the plan](practices/practice-choosing-decisive-questions.md) | You generate many questions but struggle to identify which answers matter most. |
| [Prepare checked hints for AI tutoring](practices/prepare-checked-hints-for-ai-tutoring.md) | An educator is preparing AI-supported practice on material already introduced, and can supply correct solutions and feedback. This is a bounded adaptation of a studied mathematics-tutoring package. |
| [Rehearse a bounded reasoning case](practices/rehearse-a-bounded-reasoning-case.md) | You want practice following an argument in a familiar work setting before facing a real decision. |
| [Rehearse taking over from automation](practices/rehearse-taking-over-from-automation.md) | A workflow relies on a person to detect a failure and resume control. Use a safe simulation or training environment appropriate to that workflow. |
| [Retrieve again after a delay](practices/retrieve-again-after-a-delay.md) | You need to remember or use material later, and you have a reliable source or checked solution for feedback. The authors recommend spaced study and retrieval; applying them around AI use is editorial. |
| [Turn creative disagreement into your own checklist](practices/turn-creative-disagreement-into-your-own-checklist.md) | Repeated feedback conversations reveal recurring choices in your writing, music or other craft, and you want something you can use without reopening AI. |

A checked artifact, consensus or successful outcome alone does not establish improved judgment.

| Practice | Use when |

| [Answer questions about a belief you hold](practices/answer-questions-about-your-belief.md) | You want to examine a claim you keep defending, without asking AI to decide what you should believe. |
| [Ask advisers who know the subject and you](practices/ask-contextual-human-advisers.md) | Generic advice leaves you uncertain about a real choice. |
| [Check missing perspectives against real evidence](practices/check-missing-perspectives.md) | An AI summary treats a varied group as if everyone had the same circumstances. |
| [Collect independent views before discussion](practices/collect-independent-views-before-discussion.md) | A meeting risks turning the first confident opinion into the group’s apparent view. |
| [Define the decision before asking AI to research](practices/define-the-decision-before-research.md) | You keep collecting broad briefings but cannot say what they should change. |
| [Map a return path before choosing](practices/map-a-return-path.md) | A career or work choice feels irreversible and you need to distinguish actual constraints from assumptions. |
| [Present projections with assumptions and alternatives](practices/present-projections-with-assumptions-and-alternatives.md) | People are using an AI-supported model or simulation to discuss an uncertain future. Someone can explain what the model assumes and what its output does not establish. |
| [Return to real user accounts when AI ideas distract](practices/return-to-real-user-accounts.md) | A new AI launch or competitor feature is pulling the team away from the people it serves. |
| [Seek contrary evidence in customer material](practices/seek-contrary-customer-evidence.md) | You have a favored strategy and actual interviews or feedback you can use. |
| [Turn a premortem into actions and stopping criteria](practices/turn-a-premortem-into-actions.md) | An important launch has risks people hesitate to raise. |
| [Verify reference identity and claim support](practices/verify-reference-identity-and-claim-support.md) | A consequential claim depends on a reference supplied by AI or another writer. You need to establish both that the source exists and that it supports the claim. |

<a id="calibration"></a>

## Calibration

Doubt, felt reassurance and numerical confidence ratings alone are not calibration.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|

| [Match confidence to the evidence you have](practices/match-confidence-to-evidence.md) | A proposal looks persuasive and the team must decide how much to invest. |
| [Test AI on the work it will do](practices/test-ai-on-the-work-it-will-do.md) | You need to choose or assess a model for a task you can evaluate. |

Formal approval, imitation of a voice or a disclosure log alone is insufficient.

| [Choose the result before the tool](practices/choose-the-result-before-the-tool.md) | A tool makes individual tasks faster, but the important project is not moving. Newport proposes judging progress at the level of valuable work. |
| [Keep one thinking step for yourself](practices/keep-one-thinking-step.md) | A recurring task is easier to delegate, but you want to retain a specific form of judgment, care or skill practice. |

| [Review the human work left after automation](practices/review-the-human-work-left-after-automation.md) | A team is changing a recurring job with AI. | [Save your own starting point](practices/save-your-own-starting-point.md) | You want AI to extend or critique your ideas while keeping a visible record of what you thought before its suggestions. |
| [Settle the strategy before requesting a draft](practices/settle-the-strategy-before-requesting-a-draft.md) | You are the accountable professional seeking help with an approach you can judge. |

Spend attention deliberately on chosen work and stop or redirect when appropriate.

Screen time, discomfort and task throughput alone are not the outcome.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|

Simulated audience reactions, persuasion and consensus do not establish shared understanding.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Rehearse a talk by compressing and expanding it](practices/accordion-speaking-rehearsal.md) | You know the topic but the talk is cluttered or hard to deliver without a script. |

| [Explain the other side before deciding](practices/argue-the-other-side-before-deciding.md) | A team is stuck between competing priorities. |
| [Check what each person thinks was decided](practices/check-what-each-person-thinks-was-decided.md) | A meeting is ending and a tidy summary may conceal different understandings. |

| [Use a prototype to brief a developer](practices/use-a-prototype-to-brief-a-developer.md) | You want to make an idea concrete for discussion with a developer who can assess production needs. |

## Accountability and informed participation

Make authority, contributions, data use and unresolved responsibilities inspectable to those affected.

Documentation or named ownership alone does not show agency, fairness or compliance.

| Practice | Use when |
|---|---|
| [Ask how workplace disclosures will be used](practices/ask-before-workplace-data-disclosure.md) | An onboarding or workplace assistant asks for personal or contextual information. |

| [Expand agent authority one tested step at a time](practices/expand-agent-authority-one-tested-step-at-a-time.md) | A team considers moving an assistant from suggestions toward actions. |
| [Keep a document and question tracker](practices/keep-a-document-and-question-tracker.md) | Several official documents need organizing before questions go to an accountable person or organization. |
| [Keep a record of how the writing developed](practices/keep-a-writing-process-record.md) | You want an inspectable account of assistance, research and editorial choices. |
| [Make AI disclosure specific and reciprocal](practices/make-ai-disclosure-reciprocal.md) | You use AI in work judged by others or write a policy governing their use. |
| [Name consequences before delegating](practices/name-consequences-before-delegating.md) | You are deciding whether AI should act on something you care about, rather than only draft a suggestion. |
| [Preserve an AI claim before challenging it](practices/preserve-an-ai-claim-before-challenging-it.md) | An assistant makes a surprising claim and starts changing its explanation. |
| [Record the human work needed around AI](practices/record-human-interventions.md) | Important corrections or contextual decisions disappear from the visible output. |

## Quality of the immediate work

Make a specific output, process or check more accurate, useful or fit for its purpose.

Better assisted work does not by itself show stronger thinking or retained capability.

|---|---|

| [Inspect real errors before automating evaluation](practices/inspect-real-errors-before-automating-evaluation.md) | An AI product has real interactions and domain expertise available for review. |
| [Preview and approve before publishing](practices/preview-and-approve-before-publishing.md) | AI helps produce a public video, presentation or other artifact. |
| [Reset a drifting conversation](practices/reset-a-drifting-conversation.md) | Repeated corrections have obscured the intended task. |
| [Review AI code against independent checks](practices/review-ai-code-against-independent-checks.md) | You can assess the code and expected behavior, or work with someone who can. |
| [Review quality through a real user journey](practices/review-quality-through-a-real-journey.md) | Separate good-looking parts may combine into a confusing experience. |
| [Review survey questions against the decision](practices/review-survey-questions-for-decisions.md) | A survey must yield interpretable answers rather than a number that looks useful. |
| [Test and share a team AI workflow](practices/test-and-share-a-team-ai-workflow.md) | Useful AI experiments remain private, or a team is copying prompts without understanding where they fail. |
