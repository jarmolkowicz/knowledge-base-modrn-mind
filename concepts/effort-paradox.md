# Effort Paradox

## What It Is

Effort is often costly and avoided, yet sometimes increases the value of an activity or its outcome. These effects can coexist.

## Why It Matters

[Inference] AI can remove unwanted burden while also changing the experience of participation. Ask what effort contributes, not merely whether it is hard.

## Key Insight

Inzlicht, Shenhav and Olivola (2018) distinguish value attached to an effort-produced outcome from value attached to effort itself. Evidence is stronger for the former. Valuation can arise after effort, during it or in anticipation.

More effort does not always add more value. Excessive demands, unrewarded work and salient easier alternatives can weaken or reverse the association. Subjective value does not prove improved learning or objective quality.

This non-AI review does not test AI-driven skill loss or justify making tasks unnecessarily difficult.
