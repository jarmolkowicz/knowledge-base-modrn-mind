## Summary

The paper considers how robotization could support or weaken purpose, relationships, skills and self-development, self-esteem/recognition and autonomy. Its central question is the quality and meaning of work that remains, not only the number of jobs.

The authors call for empirical case studies and further ethical analysis. Illustrative workplace stories are not original experiments, and embodied robotics should not be treated as interchangeable with conversational GenAI.
