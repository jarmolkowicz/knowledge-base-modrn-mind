# Candidate practices from the existing KB

Screening date: 2026-09-27. **Ten candidate families, not ten approved practices.** All require checking the original source before extraction. A source, a method and a concept based on that source are one evidence trail, not independent confirmations.

The procedure kernels below are paraphrases or explicitly marked editorial adaptations. No new practice cards or taxonomy changes are approved by this document.

## N01. Return later and retrieve before reopening the answer

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [bjork-desirable-difficulties-2011](<../../sources/bjork-desirable-difficulties-2011.md>); [desirable-difficulty](<../../concepts/desirable-difficulty.md>).

**Where to look:** Bjork: Key Insight and learning conditions; original-page pointers retained in the KB entry.

**Procedure lead:** [Inference] Revisit material after a delay, attempt recall or a problem without the assistant or notes, check against a reliable answer, and revisit errors later.

**Evidence basis:** Research synthesis on retrieval and spacing, predating generative AI. The learning principles have research support; this combined AI-use routine is an adaptation.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** Adds a repeated, delayed learning schedule to existing immediate attempts and capability checks.

**Limits:** Do not invent a universal interval, prescribe difficulty without prerequisites, or promise workplace transfer.

**Before extraction:** Inspect original passages on spacing and retrieval; keep the source's conditions separate from the proposed AI-use sequence.

**Existing practices to compare:** [check-a-capability-without-the-assistant](<../../practices/check-a-capability-without-the-assistant.md>); [ask-about-the-gap-then-do-the-task](<../../practices/ask-about-the-gap-then-do-the-task.md>).

## N02. Mix problem types and choose the approach yourself

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [bjork-desirable-difficulties-2011](<../../sources/bjork-desirable-difficulties-2011.md>); [desirable-difficulty](<../../concepts/desirable-difficulty.md>).

**Where to look:** Bjork: interleaving versus blocked practice.

**Procedure lead:** [Inference] Mix checked examples from related problem types; identify the required approach before solving; compare errors and receive feedback.

**Evidence basis:** Research synthesis on interleaving. Application to AI-generated exercises needs adaptation and reliable checking.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** Trains discrimination between approaches across cases, rather than reasoning through one case.

**Limits:** Interleaving is not random variety. Beginners may need instruction first. Neither generated cases nor answer keys are automatically reliable.

**Before extraction:** Check the original examples and task boundaries; decide whether a distinct card or a named learning variant is clearer.

**Existing practices to compare:** [rehearse-a-bounded-reasoning-case](<../../practices/rehearse-a-bounded-reasoning-case.md>).

## N03. Build tutoring around checked hints and learner attempts

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [bastani-guardrails-math-rct-2025](<../../sources/bastani-guardrails-math-rct-2025.md>); [zone-of-proximal-development](<../../concepts/zone-of-proximal-development.md>).

**Where to look:** Bastani: GPT Tutor configuration, teacher-supplied material, practice and unaided exam results.

**Procedure lead:** Use an educator-configured tutor with checked solutions and common mistakes, constrain help toward hints, and assess later work without assistance. Exact configuration must be recovered from the original before drafting.

**Evidence basis:** A directly studied package in high-school mathematics; the package combined several features. Its components were not isolated.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** An educator's preparation and supervision procedure, not just a learner's request for an explanation.

**Limits:** The Tutor condition had no detectable same-session exam difference from control. This is not proof of equivalence, durable learning gains, or that a 'hints only' prompt alone prevents harm.

**Before extraction:** Recover the intervention materials and assessment timing; draft within that scope and mark any workplace adaptation.

**Existing practices to compare:** [ask-about-the-gap-then-do-the-task](<../../practices/ask-about-the-gap-then-do-the-task.md>); [explain-it-back-before-the-correction](<../../practices/explain-it-back-before-the-correction.md>).

## N04. Verify a reference, then verify what it supports

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [reference-verification](<../../methods/reference-verification.md>); [topaz-fabricated-citations-2026](<../../sources/topaz-fabricated-citations-2026.md>).

**Where to look:** Reference verification: numbered procedure; Topaz: citation-audit design and limits.

**Procedure lead:** Resolve identifiers; compare title, authors and publication metadata; search alternative records when unresolved; then open the source and check whether it supports the claim.

**Evidence basis:** The method adapts a bibliographic audit. Reading for claim support is an explicitly editorial extension, not the audit's evaluated outcome.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** Checks source identity before checking an answer against it. The existing practice assumes usable reference material.

**Limits:** An unresolved identifier does not establish fabrication. A real citation does not establish that the surrounding claim is true.

**Before extraction:** Retain the audit's verification rules and uncertainty labels; separate metadata matching from substantive reading. Keep the method as the explanatory parent.

**Existing practices to compare:** [check-correctness-and-completeness](<../../practices/check-correctness-and-completeness.md>).

## N05. Check the relationships and limits in an analogy

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [gentner-markman-analogy-1997](<../../sources/gentner-markman-analogy-1997.md>); [analogical-reasoning](<../../concepts/analogical-reasoning.md>).

**Where to look:** Gentner and Markman: structural consistency, relational focus and systematicity.

**Procedure lead:** [Inference] Map which roles and relationships correspond; distinguish surface resemblance from structure; list where the mapping fails; verify any new factual inference independently.

**Evidence basis:** A theoretical synthesis of analogy and similarity. This AI-assisted checking sequence is an editorial adaptation.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** Examines the structure of one analogy, rather than asking for an opposing argument.

**Limits:** A coherent analogy can still be false or irrelevant. The source does not test this checklist with language models.

**Before extraction:** Check the original operations and examples; label the sequence as proposed and include an example with a broken mapping.

**Existing practices to compare:** [build-the-opposing-case-yourself](<../../practices/build-the-opposing-case-yourself.md>).

## N06. Rehearse taking over when automation fails

**Route:** candidate.

**KB trail:** [bainbridge-ironies-automation-1983](<../../sources/bainbridge-ironies-automation-1983.md>); [partial-automation-principle](<../../concepts/partial-automation-principle.md>); [passalacqua-less-ai-2024](<../../sources/passalacqua-less-ai-2024.md>); [parasuraman-riley-automation-1997](<../../sources/parasuraman-riley-automation-1997.md>).

**Where to look:** Bainbridge: manual skill, knowledge, process context, monitoring and training recommendations.

**Procedure lead:** [Inference] Choose a credible failure; rehearse detecting it and taking over in a suitable simulation; check whether the person has the information, skill, time and authority needed; change the design where takeover is unrealistic.

**Evidence basis:** Historical human-factors synthesis with training recommendations. Later partial-automation research offers task-specific context, not validation of this combined routine.

**Potentially distinct contribution:** Checks actual recovery and operational readiness, rather than only unaided performance or anticipated risks.

**Limits:** Simulator fidelity must fit the skill. Unknown failures cannot all be rehearsed. Neither less automation nor periodic manual work is universally better.

**Before extraction:** Recover the original recommendations; distinguish rehearsal from system-design requirements and specify a safe setting.

**Existing practices to compare:** [check-a-capability-without-the-assistant](<../../practices/check-a-capability-without-the-assistant.md>); [turn-a-premortem-into-actions](<../../practices/turn-a-premortem-into-actions.md>).

**Procedure lead:** [Inference] Map the proposed human role after automation; discuss opportunities to use and develop skills, own a meaningful contribution, make decisions, receive feedback and work with others; redesign specific losses with affected workers.
