# Review quality through a real user journey

## Use When

Separate good-looking parts may combine into a confusing experience.

## Try It

1. Choose an important end-to-end user journey and its responsible people.
2. Walk through it together across product, design and engineering.
3. Record friction and examples; assess them against explicit qualitative criteria.
4. Discuss differing judgments, decide fixes and revisit the experience.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** For AI-assisted artifacts, adapt the journey to the real reader or user task. This transfer has not been evaluated in the source.
