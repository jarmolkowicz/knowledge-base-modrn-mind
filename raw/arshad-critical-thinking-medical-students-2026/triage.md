## Summary
Arshad and colleagues survey 480 undergraduate medical students about AI use, cognitive load, self-regulated learning, and critical thinking. Their moderated mediation model links AI use positively with self-reported critical thinking and self-regulated learning, while cognitive load is negatively associated with both.

The study suggests that self-regulation may buffer the relationship between cognitive load and critical thinking. Because the design is cross-sectional and based on self-report, it cannot establish that AI use caused better critical thinking.
