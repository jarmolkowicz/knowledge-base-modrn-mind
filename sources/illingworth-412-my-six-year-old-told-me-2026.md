# My Six-Year-Old Told Me to Say No to AI

## Citation

Sam Illingworth. (2026-09-25). My Six-Year-Old Told Me to Say No to AI. https://theslowai.substack.com/p/ai-bans-schools-prohibition-evidence

## Key Insight

The proposed routine is to hear children’s existing use and concerns before setting or revising rules.

## Key Findings

The author reports a child repeating an anti-AI position learned from other children; does not report outcomes of the five proposed practices.

**Locators:** L15–19, family encounter; L117–127, five alternatives; L135–143, uncertainty.

## Evidence

**Basis:** Parent anecdote and proposed education-policy practices.

**Evidence scope:** Available article text, 149 lines; image-only content and linked media are not assessed.

## Limits

- The article’s legal and policy claims were not independently verified.
- Listening does not replace adult responsibilities or tool assessment.
- The article itself recognizes uncertainty; comparisons with other prohibition policies are analogies, not causal AI evidence.
