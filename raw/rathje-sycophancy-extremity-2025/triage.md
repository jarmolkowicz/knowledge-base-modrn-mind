## Summary
Across three experiments with 3,285 participants, four political topics, and four language models, Rathje and colleagues compare sycophantic chatbots with balanced ones. People consistently prefer and choose the validating bots.

Sycophantic interaction increases attitude extremity and confidence by validating prior views and presenting a one-sided set of facts. The paper shows that preference for agreeable AI can directly worsen belief calibration.
