# Keep a record of how the writing developed

## Use When

You want an inspectable account of assistance, research and editorial choices.

## Try It

1. Keep version history and dated early drafts while writing.
2. Retain raw notes, outlines and the research trail.
3. For each AI session, record date, tool, task, what you accepted and what you rejected.
4. Ask collaborators to describe their contribution and assistance; make your public statement match the record.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Keep a proportionate record inside authorized storage; distinguish documentation from proof or a legal guarantee.
