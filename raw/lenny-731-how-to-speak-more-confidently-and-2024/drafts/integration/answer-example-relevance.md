# Answer, example, relevance

## Use When

You need to answer a substantive question clearly and explain why the answer matters.

## Try It

1. Listen to the actual question.
2. Give a direct answer.
3. Add a concrete example and describe its relevance to the listener.
4. Practice with another person and ask whether you answered their question.

**[Inference] Editorial adaptation / boundary:** Use AI only as an optional question generator; do the answering yourself. Keep short factual questions short.
